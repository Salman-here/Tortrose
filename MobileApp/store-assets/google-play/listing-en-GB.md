## Short description

## Full description

Rozare brings marketplace shopping, independent sellers and helpful AI assistance together in one polished mobile experience.

SHOP WITH CONFIDENCE

• Discover products from independent sellers and brands
• Browse categories, stores, trusted stores and featured products
• Choose required product options such as size or colour before adding to cart
• Save favourites to your wishlist and keep your cart in sync
• Apply eligible coupons and choose from the checkout methods available to you
• Follow order progress from confirmation through delivery
• Review delivered purchases and stores

GET HELP FROM ROZARE AI

• Search naturally for the products you need
• Ask questions from a product page before you buy
• Get product suggestions, comparisons and shopping guidance
• Use text or voice and return to previous chat sessions
• Report an AI response whenever something does not look right

DISCOVER STORES YOU CAN TRUST

Explore seller storefronts, product details, ratings, reviews and trust indicators. Report inappropriate products, reviews, stores or sellers, and block accounts you no longer want to see.

SELL AND MANAGE ON THE GO

Sellers can create and manage products, product options, stock, orders, shipping methods, coupons, store details and customer workflows. Seller tools also include analytics, alerts and supported AI or WhatsApp-assisted workflows.

MADE FOR EVERYDAY SHOPPING

• Light, dark and system appearance modes
• Multi-currency display support
• Push notifications for important order and account updates
• Saved addresses for faster checkout
• Account privacy controls and in-app account deletion

Rozare is a general shopping marketplace. Product availability, shipping, payment methods and seller services can vary by location and seller.
